AgentBar: first launch
======================

1. Drag AgentBar into the Applications folder.

2. Open it once. AgentBar isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS says it
   "could not verify AgentBar is free of malware". That's expected.
   Approve it once:

   macOS 13 Ventura and 14 Sonoma
     • In Applications, Control-click (or right-click) AgentBar, choose Open, then Open.

   macOS 15 Sequoia and later
     • Double-click AgentBar. macOS blocks it; click Done.
     • Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
     • Scroll to "AgentBar was blocked", click Open Anyway, and enter your password.

   In Terminal, instead:
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/AgentBar.app

3. AgentBar appears in the menu bar (top right of your screen) and opens
   a short setup guide: allow alerts, open at login, connect Claude Code,
   and try a sample request.

Check your download: run  shasum -a 256 AgentBar-*.dmg  and compare it with
SHA256SUMS.txt published next to the download.
